Laxmi Agro Enterprises may collect the following information to provide its wholesale agricultural-equipment marketplace and support services:

ACCOUNT DELETION AND RETENTION

You can request deletion of your Laxmi Agro account from Account & Privacy in the app or at https://www.laxmiagroenterprises.com/delete-account.

After staff verifies the request, we target completion within 30 days. Direct account data is deleted or anonymized, including profile details, saved addresses, uploaded account media, carts, device tokens, notification history, and negotiations.

Orders and payment records may be retained in restricted records when required for tax, payment, fraud-prevention, dispute, warranty, or other legal obligations. Backup handling follows our applicable operational and legal retention requirements.
